The Fellowship working group formally nominates community members for a fellowship role, according to the Erlang Ecosystem Foundation bylaws. Meet the current [EEF Fellows](/fellows/).

## Mission

- Formally nominate community members for a fellowship role according to the Erlang Ecosystem Foundation bylaws.
- Create and maintain the criteria and procedure for the nomination of fellows.

## Objectives

- Nominate fellows to the Foundation.
- Hold yearly fellowship nominations.

## Benefits to the community

- Recognize the efforts of long-term community members and participants.
- Provide the Foundation with the input of those members.

## Why the group belongs to the Foundation

- The group is mandated by the bylaws.
- The group defines the criteria for an entire member category of the Foundation.
