We will start out with introductions. If you’re shy, don’t worry, you can just hang out and wait until you are more comfortable.

Then I will share my terminal window and briefly talk about my development environment.

To get started I would like to work through an Exercism exercise, and test if my dev environment is working as it should.

Finally any members can bring up any coding challenges that they have and we can try to mob-solve them.

And if anyone wants to volunteer to do a presentation or lighting talk we can schedule it.

I’m happy to discuss any tangent topics including git workflows, other dev environments, launching projects into production, devops around supporting Elixir/Erlang and the BEAM, and so on.

And we might want to talk about meeting in Real Life, either at one of the 3-D meetups like Geeks Who Drink, or for far away visitors, possibly at a future conference event.
