About Us

Ohio Elixir is a community group comprised of anyone and everyone interested in the Elixir ecosystem. We host monthly meetings where guest speakers present Elixir-related content, as well as occasional show and tell nights where anyone can talk about what they’ve been working on or ask questions.

At this time we are a fully remote organization which gives us the unique opportunity to open our virtual doors to folks outside of Ohio!

Join Us

Join us this month for a show-and-tell style event where you can hear about what other people are working on or share something that interests you! The event will kick off at 6:00 PM EDT but feel free to join early and chat with others.

Find more information at <https://www.ohioelixir.com/> Or join our discord and start chatting <https://discord.gg/JVVSwSNpK6>
