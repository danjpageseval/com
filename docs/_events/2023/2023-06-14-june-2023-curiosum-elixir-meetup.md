# Speakers

Check speakers & topics of our upcoming meetup!

## Michael Lubas

**How to Learn Elixir Security**

Michael is the founder of Paraxial.io, a company that secures Elixir and Phoenix applications. He has several years of experience working in web application security, software development, and security consulting. He wants to see every Elixir and Phoenix developer have the tools they need to keep their software safe.

PRESENTATION How to Learn Elixir Security

There are many educational resources for learning web application security today, however only a few focus on Elixir and Phoenix. This talk will cover the current educational resources available on Elixir, what topics in security are most relevant for Elixir developers, and how businesses should craft their security strategy for Elixir.

## Zvonimir Rudinski

**Challenges and procedures when dealing with an open-source project - Tenantee**

Zvonimir is a talented full-stack developer who is currently holding the position of Technical Lead at Noubis. If you have any questions about Elixir, JS or TypeScript he is your guy to ask. Right now, he is in the middle of working on his open-source project called Tenantee. On a more personal note, besides being a programming polyglot, he is passionate about gardening and cooking. Always looking for new solutions to new problems - this is his way of improving and getting experience in life. Lots of projects and lots of work but he will always find time to learn new things and build something great.
