About GOTO Aarhus GOTO Aarhus Summit is a series of three summits covering; Cyber Security, Green Tech, and Investment in Cyber Security Startups. Read more about each of the summits below:

May 24: Cyber Security Summit Cyber Security Summit will gather experts in the field of cyber security to discuss the latest trends and threats. The event will consist of a series of talks by experts from the industry focusing on both global and national challenges within cyber protection. Furthermore, looking into the barriers of implementing cyber security and if companies in general are prepared for what’s coming.

Join us for this summit and get to network with other like-minded professionals within cyber security.

May 25: Green Tech Summit Green Tech Summit is focusing on the high demand for digitalization and how technology can contribute to a more sustainable environment. At this event talks you will get to hear about how green code, the new term “digitainability” and how we should mentally prepare for the green technology. Lastly, you will get a guided tour to see a Smart Building and how this contributes to a more sustainable world.

Join us for the summit and expand your knowledge within green tech while networking with fellow attendees.

May 26: Cyber Security Startup Investor Summit The Cyber Security Startup Investor Summit will bring together experts in the field of cyber security to discuss the latest trends, opportunities, and challenges facing startups and investors in this dynamic market. From emerging technologies to emerging cyber threats, our experts and startups will share insights on how to make the most of this rapidly evolving field of technological and financial opportunity space.

The event will feature a series of presentations/pitches by startups highlighting different trends, a panel discussion assessing the key trends in the cyber security investment opportunity, and networking opportunities, providing great opportunities to connect with other like-minded professionals.

Whether you’re a startup looking for funding or an investor seeking opportunities in the cyber security sector, or just generally interested in the space - this event is designed to help you stay ahead of the curve.

Key topics that will be covered at the event include:

The latest trends and challenges in cyber security for startups and investors Emerging technologies and innovative solutions for addressing cyber threats Strategies for navigating the complex landscape of cyber security Key trends to be explored at the event: Cloud Security: With more and more data being stored on the cloud, securing it has become a top priority for organisations. Cloud security solutions such as encryption, access control, and threat intelligence are increasingly being adopted.

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: AI and machine learning are being used to analyse large volumes of data to detect and respond to cyber threats. These technologies can help organisations detect threats faster and improve response times.

IoT Security: The increasing number of internet-connected devices has led to a rise in IoT-based cyber attacks. To prevent these attacks, organisations need to adopt robust security measures and implement regular software updates.

Identity and Access Management: Ensuring the security of user identities and access privileges is crucial to prevent unauthorised access to sensitive data. Identity and access management solutions are being widely adopted to address this challenge.

Cyber Threat Intelligence: Cyber threat intelligence provides organisations with insights into the latest threats and vulnerabilities. By using this intelligence to proactively defend against cyber threats, organisations can minimise the risk of a successful attack.
