For over a decade, Lambda Days has united the worlds of functional programming in academia and industry. This fusion of research and real-world applications continues to spark fresh ideas - no matter your level of expertise.

Whether you’re a first-time attendee or a familiar face, we’re thrilled to explore the future of functional programming with you!

Join us in beautiful, sunny Krakow to push the boundaries of what’s possible. Dive into battle-tested Scala, Erlang, and Haskell, feel the energy of F#, Elixir or Gleam, connect with the minds shaping Elm, Luna, and OCaml - and discover what comes next!
