Elixir Curitiba 2025 will take place in Curitiba, Paraná, Brazil, on 8 November 2025, bringing the BEAM community together once again for a full day dedicated to the Elixir language and the Erlang ecosystem. The event will be held at Banco Senff – Av. Sen. Souza Naves, 1240 – Cristo Rei, Curitiba – PR, Brazil, 80050-152.

As in 2024, the event will feature inspiring talks, exchanges of experiences, networking opportunities, and a welcoming environment that values both those who already have experience with functional programming and those who are taking their first steps. The main language of the event will be Portuguese, facilitating the participation of the local community.

In 2024, the event brought together developers, enthusiasts, and researchers, offering rich moments of learning and collaboration. For 2025, we hope to repeat and expand on this success, further strengthening the Elixir community in Curitiba and Brazil.
