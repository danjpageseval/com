> Host The next BEAM Languages United meetup is hosted by Erlang Solutions.

> Agenda

17.00 Start registration 17.20 Intro 17.30 Peer Stritzinger - TBA (20 min + Q&A) 18.00 Snacks 18.30 Ulf Wiger - Community Mining on the Gajumaru (20 min + Q&A) 19.00 Lars Wikman - My Nerves - A Gadget Tour (20 min + Q&A) 19.30 Final mingle 20.00 Optional: Join us for a drink at a nearby pub/bar

> Talk 1: Peer Stritzinger, Erlang - more info to come

> Talk 2: Ulf Wiger, Erlang + Elixir - Community Mining on the Gajumaru How would you design a crypto mining pool infrastructure to scale towards millions of miners? On BEAM, of course, but also, why? We at QPQ are doing this for the Gajumaru - an actual blockchain that actually works. In this talk, we will briefly describe how Gajumaru is different, and how we have approached the task of offering controlled mining for world-wide adoption.

> Talk 3: Lars Wikman, Elixir - My Nerves - A Gadget Tour In this show-and-tell I will demonstrate fiscal irresponsibility, fun hobbyist hardware and serious embedded gear from a Nerves perspective. We’ll touch on NervesHub, various Nerves systems and even a real Nerves product or two.

> Any allergies or special requirements? We plan to serve some snacks and soft drinks during the event. Let us know if you have any allergies or any other special requirements.
