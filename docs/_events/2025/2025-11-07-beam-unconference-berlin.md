​Let’s unconference before you go! To top off a beaming week, the Erlang Ecosystem Foundation, Alembic and bitcrowd are joining up to bring us together for an organic yet slightly organized attendee driven event. We already know some Ash core contributors will be on hand.

​The format for this will be a kickoff round of five minute lightning talks. From them, we will select topics people want to hear more about, discuss or collectively hack on. Depending on the votes, there may be a mix of single and double tracks.
