### Jan Henry Nystrom, Erlang - Yet Another Mocking Library for Erlang

A presentation of a mocking library for Erlang that has a slightly different take on mocking than the existing libs and rational for these differences. Background, overview and examples of use.

### Talk 2: Brian Underwood, Elixir - My Elixir Hot Takes

I’ll present some of the more interesting Elixir perspectives I’ve developed through years of hands-on experience with the language. Ranging from syntax preferences to architectural approaches. Consider it a friendly challenge to think differently about familiar concepts.
