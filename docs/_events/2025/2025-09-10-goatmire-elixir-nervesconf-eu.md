A small-scale Elixir conference put together for the Elixir programming community by the Underjord team. By Elixir enthusiasts for Elixir enthusiasts. The event will be run by a small not-for-profit we’ve created. It is located in the seaside town of Varberg, a pretty place with unreasonably good food and a nice walkable city center conveniently accessible by train from Copenhagen or Gothenburg. Influenced by smaller flavorful events like GigCity Elixir, The Big Elixir and EMPEX this will be Sweden’s first Elixir conference.
