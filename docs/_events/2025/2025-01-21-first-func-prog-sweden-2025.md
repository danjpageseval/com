Welcome to the first Func Prog Sweden MeetUp 2025 – Online

Local-first software development with Elixir This talk introduces ElectricSQL, a sync engine developed in Elixir, and walks through how you can use it with existing web frameworks like Phoenix to develop local-first software.

James Arthur <https://www.linkedin.com/in/thruflo/> <https://electric-sql.com> <https://hexdocs.pm/electric_phoenix/Electric.Phoenix.html>

Transforming Composition One of the common “gateways” to getting hooked on FP is when developers discover composition patterns. In JS, this often looks like chains of array method calls using map(), filter(), and reduce(). But deep in the caves below the surface, dragons (of inefficiency) lurk!

We’ll explore the caves, slaying those dragons as we uncover various ways of transforming our compositions in JS, including generators and transducers.

Kyle Simpson Kyle Simpson is a web-oriented software engineer, widely acclaimed for his “You Don’t Know JS” book series and nearly 1M hours viewed of his online courses. Kyle’s superpower is asking better questions, and he deeply believes in maximally using the minimally-necessary tools for any task. As a “human-centric technologist”, he’s passionate about bringing humans and technology together, evolving engineering organizations towards solving the right problems, in simpler ways. Kyle will always fight for the people behind the pixels. <https://me.getify.com> <https://github.com/getify> <https://byojs.dev/> <https://www.linkedin.com/in/getify/>
