🎅 🎅 🎅 Ho Ho Ho, it’s Christmas edition again! 🎅 🎅 🎅

It is this time of the year! The smell of gingerbread and glühwein is going around the markets and Edeka is selling chocolate Santa figures.

So you now the holiday season is coming. We want to invite the whole BEAM community to spend a cozy evening with us.

Reflect on the past year, enjoy some cookies and have fun. We will have some space for unconference style discussion of topics you would like to bring to the community. If you’ve never heard about an “unconference” before have a look at this primer: <https://agilecrete.org/what-is-an-open-space-unconference/>

But more than technical discussions the end-of-year event is always time to hang-out with the community and get into the the mood.
