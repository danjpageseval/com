Come and join Nerves Core team member Gus Workman for this special hardware hacking event on November 3rd!

We will have a short, hands-on interactive session, perfect for both new and experienced Elixir developers.Bring a Raspberry Pi (of any flavour) for the event. We’ll be working with project Soleil, a low power HAT for Raspberry Pi devices.

This session will provide a short introduction to Nerves, hardware and running Elixir on embedded devices. We’ll also have a discussion about upcoming developments in the Nerves ecosystem.

Pizza and drinks will be provided!
