The premiere Elixir conference in Europe!

To make sure everyone feels safe and comfortable, we’re going to run a HYBRID event, combining both an in-person and virtual conference. Read more about it here.

There are two different categories of tickets going on sale at the same time - for the in-person conference (available here) and for virtual conference and training attendance (available on this website).

The conference is a melting pot for new ideas and inspired thinking. It showcases the great work being done with Elixir by individuals and companies alike. It helps those new to the community find their feet, and welcomes back those who have attended before, to champion and progress the uptake and development of Elixir in Europe.
