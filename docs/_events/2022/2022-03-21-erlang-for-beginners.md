Learn the language that carries 90% of the world’s internet traffic. This five-day introductory course is a hands-on way to learn the basic principles and get you feeling comfortable with the language. Our courses are instructor-led and perfect for beginners, who’ll have the ability to pair program and ask questions as and when they need. This course is the perfect stepping stone for you to start working with Erlang to enable you to use it in commercial projects.
