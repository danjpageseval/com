This is our second virtual conference after hosting 400 Elixir users from around the world in June. The conference is a melting pot for new ideas and inspired thinking. It showcases the great work being done with Elixir by individuals and companies alike. To help inspire and reach new members of the community, the Erlang Ecosystem Foundation has sponsored the students of the Uni of Kaiserslauten to attend the conference and tutorials.
