Join us for an in-depth exploration of “Basket,” a template app designed for Phoenix Presence implementations. This session, led by our speaker, Dave, will delve into the application of WebSocket stock ticker messages distribution as a refreshing deviation from the typical chat server app use case.

Dave will begin by walking us through best practices in Phoenix Presence, highlighting key differences and advantages of these implementations over conventional methods. The session will also cover the “autosuggest” search feature in Basket, demonstrating the integration of RESTful features into LiveView.

Finally, he will share insights into effective testing strategies with ExUnit using the Adapter pattern. He’ll provide practical examples from the repository, offering attendees a hands-on perspective: <https://www.github.com/daveminer/basket>

Don’t miss this opportunity to elevate your understanding of Phoenix Presence and engage with fellow enthusiasts.

We encourage participants to familiarize themselves with the repository prior to the event for a more enriching experience. Looking forward to an interactive and informative session!
