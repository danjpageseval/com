It is this time of the year! The smell of gingerbread and glühwein is going around the markets and Edeka is selling chocolate Santa figures. So you now Christmas is coming. We want to invite the whole BEAM community to spend a cozy evening with us. Reflect on the past year, enjoy some cookies and have fun. We will have some space for unconference style discussion of topics you would like to bring to the community. If you’ve never heard about an “unconference” before have a look at this primer: <https://agilecrete.org/what-is-an-open-space-unconference/>

But more than technical discussions the Christmas event is always time to hang-out with the community and get into the the mood.

We’re still preparing the Agenda, and this is the current draft:

This is the Christmas edition, so there will be some Glühwein and space and time to just chat and relax! See you all there
