🎄 You’re Invited to the Zagreb Elixir Advent Drinkup! 🎄

​Join us for a festive gathering of Elixir enthusiasts right in the heart of Zagreb’s Advent season! Whether you’re diving into Elixir for the first time or you’re a seasoned developer, this is a fantastic opportunity to share knowledge, swap stories, and catch up on the latest in the Elixir community—all with some holiday spirit.

​At our last meetup, we had great discussions over beers about topics like Oban Pro, the ex_unit behavioral testing approach, using DuckDB for efficient CSV parsing, and even exploring LLM integration with Elixir! We’re excited to keep the conversation going, so bring your ideas, questions, and experiences to share.
