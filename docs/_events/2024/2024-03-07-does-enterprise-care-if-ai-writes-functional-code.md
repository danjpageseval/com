Introducing Joey Feldberg, a dynamic Principal Software Engineer whose expertise spans over five years at Wix, a tech powerhouse renowned for its cutting-edge backend development powered by Scala. Prior to this, Joey served as the Chief Architect for a forward-thinking company deeply rooted in Erlang, where he honed his skills in crafting robust and scalable systems.

Join us as we delve into the captivating world of functional programming alongside Joey. Together, we’ll unravel the intricacies of its relevance, navigate through its challenges, and explore its adoption within the realm of big corporations.

Through firsthand anecdotes, Joey will share his personal journey, shed light on practical applications, delve into historical context, and unveil the evolving attitudes of major companies towards this transformative paradigm.

Don’t miss this exclusive opportunity to gain insights from a seasoned expert at the forefront of functional programming innovation.
