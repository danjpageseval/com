SmartLogic’s Elixir Wizards \|\> Conference is an Elixir Phoenix community event that will take place online over two half-days: Wednesday, June 16th and Thursday, June 17th, 2021.

Like our podcast, the Elixir Wizards \|\> Conference focuses on bringing people together from across the Elixir community and evangelizing the benefits of the platform to the greater development community.

This virtual conference will feature a mix of presentation formats, including a couple of keynotes, leadership panel discussions, short format talks, and of course, some quality networking time!
