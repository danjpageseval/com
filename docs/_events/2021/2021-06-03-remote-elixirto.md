It’s “almost” summer!

Until things are safer we keep meeting online! Come join us for an evening about Elixir and community with a special event, wherever you are!

The appointment is for Thursday June 3rd, 6:30 pm, Zoom link is attached to this page.

We will start talks sooner than usual given that we won’t have a lot of space to mingle, so try to come on time if you can.

This month we’ll talk about LiveView and 3rd party SDKs!

First Eric Froese (<https://github.com/ericdude4>) will present Shopifex! Shopifex is a Phoenix boilerplate for Shopify Embedded App SDK. Installation ✓, Admin links ✓, Webhooks ✓, App billing ✓, everything else ✓. Get an Elixir Phoenix backed Shopify app up and running quickly and easily.

Shortly after, John Davenport (<https://github.com/johns10>) will be on stage with Elixtron: Implementing desktop features in LiveView apps.

If you wanna have a chat, look for our community on the \#toronto channel of the Elixir Slack <https://elixir-slackin.herokuapp.com/>

p.s. Given that this is a remote event please feel free to extend the invite outside the Toronto community as well! We’ll be happy to have you regardless of where you are!
