The OW2 open source community annual conference will take place, online, June 23-24, 2021. This year OW2con’21 theme is ‘Leveraging the European Open Source Ecosystem’. With 36 sessions, the conference will address the many challenges of ensuring a sustainable European open source ecosystem. Please, check the conference program and register for free at: <https://www.ow2con.org>
