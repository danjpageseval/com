In this talk, presented by AJ, we will learn how Elixir lays out structures in memory, and how it behaves when we transform data immutably. Then, we’ll use this knowledge to guide how we choose data structures for a real world app.

After the scheduled talk, we’ll open the floor for lightning talks. If you are interested in presenting on a short topic, or sharing something cool, this is your time! Feel free to ask for aux or hit me up directly and I’ll be more than happy to help get you set up :)
