In this session, Johan Mattisson, system developer and CEO at 111 Solutions, and Elixir enthusiast, will introduce us to the Elixir programming language. He will take us through the basic building blocks of the language, show some code examples and practical tools.

If you are developers or students curious to learn about Elixir, this session is exactly for you. If you are new to Elixir, this is the perfect time to take your first steps and get your feet wet! If you have experience with Elixir, you will feel very familiar; this session will be an opportunity for you to revisit your Elixir basics.

Welcome to you all, and spread the words to your coder friends.
