Our monthly meetings are held online and provide a casual and welcoming environment for people to share and learn from each other.

Meeting Agenda

Round the Room Introductions Say hello and share recent Elixir wins, questions, requests for help.

Group News and Photo Meta info on group events and projects.

Elixir Industry News A collection of impactful stories from around the community.

Talks / Show and Tell Presentations and discussions led by fellow members.

Code Jam Presentations

Last month we kicked off a small code jam event, asking members to work on a game module that could play a TicTacToe game and this month we will have those presentations. The goal is by having different people solve the same problem we can quickly observe different coding approaches and learn from each other. For more info check out:

<https://gist.github.com/zorn/129279df227ab984d09472f00160c932>

We have room for another talk. Let Mike know if you have something to share.

While we encourage people to participate in our meetings with video cameras on to help provide a more rich experience, we also are happy to host people who would prefer to keep their cameras off.
