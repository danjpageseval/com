Ben Mmari, a software engineer from Zappi, will discuss his experience of building out a public facing API in Elixir. In his talk he will discuss why he opted for Elixir, the challenges he faced and the learnings made while making the jump from Ruby to Elixir.

Since this is our first meetup event, there will be moderate time afterwards to ask questions and share voluntarily to get to know each other a bit better.

The meeting will be remote and the link will be posted a day before the event.

Anyone is welcome, even if you are just curious about Elixir.
