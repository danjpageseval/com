A comprehensive deep dive into RabbitMQ best practices, internal components, design and implementation. This three-day online training contains lots of hands-on and lab work and is more suited for senior engineers. This advanced training will walk you through critical RabbitMQ Erlang/OTP based internals, from aspects such boot procedures to messaging operations and components which are usually hidden from general RabbitMQ users and operators, this training will stretch your understanding of RabbitMQ to the underlying designer and implementer’s perspective. You will also learn advanced operations and maintenance techniques, which will equip you to troubleshoot and fix some of the most complex production issues which may arise on any RabbitMQ installation.
