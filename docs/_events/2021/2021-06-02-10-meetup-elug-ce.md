The Elug CE meetups are monthly virtual gatherings of Elixir developers from Ceará, Brazil. The event takes place on the first Wednesdays of each month (always 7 pm BRT), with usually 2-3 tech talks and some social talking endings. It is mostly held in Brazilian Portuguese, with some eventual English-speaking editions.

The event link is shared in our Telegram group, ~1h before the start. Everyone is welcome to join!
