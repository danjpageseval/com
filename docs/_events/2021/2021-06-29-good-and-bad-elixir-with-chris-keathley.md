DC \|\> Elixir welcomes Chris Keathley to chat about his recent blog post Good & Bad Elixir and talk about some of the Dos and Don’ts of various Elixir patterns.

There was a lot of discussion in the community about this post, so we thought it would be a fun time to get together to chat about it!
